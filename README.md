# Supply-Chain-Management-Dashboard

An end-to-end operational dashboard focused on inventory optimization, regional demand forecasting, and streamlining supply chain logistics.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed to tackle complex logistics challenges by providing a 360-degree view of the supply chain lifecycle. The primary objective was to move beyond basic tracking and focus on inventory optimization — ensuring high-demand products stay in stock while reducing carrying costs for slow-moving items.

By transforming raw logistics data into interactive visuals, I identified critical bottlenecks in regional distribution and delivered actionable insights to improve fulfillment rates. This dashboard serves as a strategic tool for stakeholders to monitor stock availability, sales trends, and regional performance in real time.

## 📂 Data Source

This project utilizes a comprehensive supply chain dataset encompassing warehouse logistics, inventory stock levels, and regional demand. The data simulates a real-world operational environment, enabling deep-dive analysis into supply-demand gaps and store/region-wise performance.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
- 📊 Power BI Desktop – Used for end-to-end data storytelling and building interactive operational dashboards.
- 🔄 Power Query (ETL) – Performed data transformation, including merging store/region data and cleaning product-level inventory data.
- 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Developed custom measures for Total Sales, Inventory Value, YOY Growth %, and Stock Status.
- 🔗 Data Modeling – Established relationships between Sales, Inventory, Product, Store, and Date tables for efficient analysis.
- 📁 File Format – .pbix (project development) and .png (performance preview).

###🔸The Business Problem:

Supply chain operations often suffer from inventory imbalances — some regions or stores are overstocked with slow-moving items, while others face critical stockouts of high-demand products. The core challenge for this project was to analyze sales and inventory data to identify these supply-demand gaps and provide a clear, real-time view of stock health across products, regions, and stores.

---

###🔸 Goal of the Dashboard:

The objective was to build an interactive analytical tool that:

• Stock Health Monitoring: Tracks Total Sales, Inventory Value, and Inventory In Hand together to maintain a healthy stock-to-sales balance.

• Stock Availability Tracking: Identifies real-time Stock Status (In Stock / Out of Stock) to prevent revenue loss from stockouts.

• Regional & Store Benchmarking: Analyzes regional, state, and store-wise performance to pinpoint high and low performing areas.

• Trend & Growth Forecasting: Identifies year-over-year growth patterns and monthly seasonal trends to support proactive planning.

• Time-Based Performance Tracking: Enables YTD, QTD, and MTD comparison for consistent performance benchmarking.

---

###🔸 Walkthrough of Key Visuals:

• Core KPI Cards (Top Section): Displays four high-level metrics — Total Sales (388.13M), Inventory Value (706.21K), Inventory In Hand (1.905K), and Stock Status (In Stock) — along with a Product Name filter dropdown. Insight: Gives managers an instant snapshot of overall sales performance and current stock health.

• Total Sales by Product Type (Donut Chart): Visualizes the contribution of different product categories (Arts & Entertainment, Photography, Music, Entertainment, Computers, Mobiles) to total revenue. Purpose: Identifies which product lines drive the most value and which are underperforming.

• YOY Growth % (Waterfall Chart): Compares year-over-year growth from 2020 to 2023, showing a rise followed by a sharp decline (-73% and -51%) leading into the total. Utility: Essential for identifying whether performance is improving or declining over time.

• Total Sales by Region (Bar Chart): Compares sales across major regions — West, South, East, Midwest, and Southwest. Insight: Helps in regional resource allocation based on demand strength.

• Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart): Tracks month-by-month sales fluctuations across the year to identify seasonality and peak demand periods.

• Top 5 Sales by State (Area Chart): Highlights the top 5 revenue-generating states — California, Florida, Texas, Massachusetts, and Alabama. Purpose: Identifies which states to prioritize for stock allocation.

• Top 5 Store Wise Sales (Bar Chart): Ranks the top 5 individual stores by sales — Tilloch, Hammersmith, Elizabeth St, Calvin, and Arthur stores. Purpose: Enables strategic store-level inventory replenishment.

• YTD, QTD, MTD Sales (Bar Chart): Provides a detailed, dynamic breakdown of Year-to-Date, Quarter-to-Date, and Month-to-Date sales performance across 2019–2021.

• Total Sales by Store Region (Bottom Summary Bar): A consolidated footer view comparing total sales across all regions — Midwest, Southwest, West, South, and East — at a glance.

• Dynamic Filters & Slicers: Interactive slicers for Years, Regions, and Payment Method (Cash, Credit, Debit, Other) enable granular, root-cause level analysis.

---

###🔸 Business Impact & Insights:

• Inventory Optimization: Balances stock levels — reducing overstock carrying costs while preventing revenue loss from stockouts.

• Strategic Regional Growth: Pinpoints high-performing regions and stores, enabling better resource allocation and targeted planning.

• Proactive Demand Planning: Identifies seasonal sales trends, shifting the business from reactive to proactive inventory replenishment.

• Store-Level Optimization: Highlights top and bottom performing stores to guide restocking priorities.

• Data-Driven Leadership: Empowers stakeholders with real-time, actionable insights, replacing gut-feeling decisions with fact-based ones.
